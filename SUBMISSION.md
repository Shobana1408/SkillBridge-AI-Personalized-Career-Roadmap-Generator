# VORTEX 2K26 — Project Submission Details

## Project title
SkillBridge AI — Personalized Career Roadmap Generator

## Theme
Future of Education & Employability

## Problem statement
Students often struggle to turn career ambitions into a realistic learning sequence. Online resources are plentiful, but a learner may not know which skills to prioritize, how to adapt a roadmap to their current level, or how to turn practice into portfolio evidence. Generic roadmaps can overwhelm students who have limited time or are starting from different skill levels.

## Proposed solution
SkillBridge AI is a lightweight career-planning web app that creates a personalized learning roadmap based on a student's target role, current skill level, weekly study hours, and timeline. It offers week-by-week tasks, estimated effort, a portfolio mini-project, progress tracking, and downloadable plans. Its built-in recommendation engine works without a paid service; an optional Gemini API mode can enhance plan generation when configured by the user.

## Key features
- Personalized learning plans for five career paths.
- Adjustable level, weekly time budget, and 2–8 week duration.
- Actionable weekly tasks and portfolio project suggestion.
- Task completion tracking and progress indicator.
- Export roadmap as a text file.
- Responsive, mobile-friendly interface.
- Optional generative AI enhancement with local-only key entry.

## Technology stack
HTML5, CSS3, vanilla JavaScript, browser localStorage, optional Google Gemini API, GitHub Pages.
